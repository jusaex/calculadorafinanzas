Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

Provisional information: this site is not currently monetised. Full legal operator details will be added before advertising is enabled.

Calculator data

Figures entered into calculators are processed locally in your browser. CalculadoraFinanzas does not receive or store them.

Direct data collection

There are no user accounts, forms, newsletters or advertising. CalculadoraFinanzas does not directly request names, email addresses, financial data or other personal information.

Web analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to obtain aggregated information about visits and page performance. According to Cloudflare, this service works without cookies, does not collect or use visitors' personal data, and does not track individual users across websites. Its measurement script loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com and sends technical metrics to Cloudflare. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service under its Privacy Statement.

Future advertising

Before Google AdSense or any non-essential tracking technology is enabled, this policy will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be introduced.
